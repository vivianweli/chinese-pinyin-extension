# Privacy Policy for Chinese Pinyin Helper

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Chinese Pinyin Helper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information. Everything happens on your device.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Text you select on web pages.** When you select Chinese text, the extension looks it up in a dictionary that is bundled inside the extension and shows the pinyin next to your selection. The selected text is never sent anywhere, saved, or logged.
- **Your setting.** The only thing the extension saves is whether you turned it on or off (a single on/off value). It is kept in your browser's local extension storage and is not synced or sent to any server.
- **Clipboard.** When you click a pinyin box, that pinyin is copied to your clipboard. The extension never reads your clipboard.

## Third parties

This extension does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, remote servers, or any third-party services. It makes no network requests. It does not sell or share any data.

## Why it runs on all websites

The extension needs to run on every page so the pinyin box can appear wherever you select Chinese text. It only acts when you select text, and it does not read or modify any other part of the page.

## Data retention and deletion

The on/off setting stays in your browser until you uninstall the extension, which removes it.
